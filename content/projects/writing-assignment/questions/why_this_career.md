## 2. Why do you want this career? [100 - 300 words]

I want to become a web developer because I enjoy creating websites and making ideas come to life through coding. 
The internet is a big part of our daily lives, and I want to be part of the people who build and improve websites that help businesses and individuals connect and grow.
My interest in web development started when I learned how websites work and realized how much creativity and problem-solving go into building them. 
I like the combination of design, functionality, and logic that web development requires. 
It allows me to think critically and create something useful at the same time.
I applied for this course because I want to improve my coding skills and gain hands-on experience in building websites and applications. 
Web development is always evolving, with new technologies and frameworks, and I want to keep learning to stay up to date in the field.
In the future, I want to work as a professional web developer, helping businesses and organizations build user-friendly and secure websites. 
I also hope to create my own projects one day, possibly starting a tech-related business. 
This career offers endless opportunities for growth, creativity, and financial stability, which is why I am excited to pursue it.
